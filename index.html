Privacy model

      
Conversion uses local browser APIs, WebAssembly, WebCodecs, workers, OPFS, PDF.js, DuckDB, SQLite, SheetJS, qpdf, Tesseract, zip.js, and libarchive. Source files, passwords, extracted text, OCR results, and outputs are not uploaded to a conversion server.
